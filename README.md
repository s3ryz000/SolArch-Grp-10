## About the study

An enterprise architecture study of the Barangay San Antonio Health Center, Pasig City. It is based on interviews and written answers from 16 health center staff and barangay officials (October 2–21, 2026), conducted under a permit from the City Health Officer, with key figures verified by the health center on October 22, 2026. No patient names or records were collected.

Every document follows the TOGAF template from the course pack and includes a table of contents, numbered pages and an appendix of abbreviations. Each document is provided as both DOCX and PDF.
