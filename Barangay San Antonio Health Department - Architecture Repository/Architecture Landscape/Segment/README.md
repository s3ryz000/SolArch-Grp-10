# Segment Architectures

This engagement is a **segment architecture**: one health center within the Pasig City health landscape. Its baseline, target and transition states are recorded in:

| Document | Location | Content |
|---|---|---|
| A5 Architecture Definition | `Barangay San Antonio Health Department - Phase A (Architecture Vision)` | Baseline, target and transition architectures across all four domains |
| B1 Architecture Deliverables (Phase B) | `Barangay San Antonio Health Department - Phase B (Business Architecture)` | Business catalogs, matrices and process flows |
| C1 Architecture Deliverables (Phase C) | `Barangay San Antonio Health Department - Phase C (Information Systems Architecture)` | Data and application artifacts |
| D1 Architecture Deliverables (Phase D) | `Barangay San Antonio Health Department - Phase D (Technology Architecture)` | Technology artifacts |

Diagrams for each phase are in that phase folder's `Diagrams` subfolder.
