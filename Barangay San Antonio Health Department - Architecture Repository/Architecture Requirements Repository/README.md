# Architecture Requirements Repository

The evidence behind the requirements: what was asked, what was found, and the figures used.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Barangay San Antonio Health Department - Interview Findings (Phases A-E)` (.docx, .pdf) | Consolidated findings from interviews and written answers of 16 health center staff and barangay officials (October 2–21, 2026), mapped to Phases A–E |
| `Barangay San Antonio Health Department - Interview Guide (Questions).pdf` | Interview instrument used with each respondent |
| `Barangay San Antonio Health Department - Final Verification Questions v2 (for Nurse Hardman).docx` | Questions used to verify key figures with the health center (October 22, 2026) |
| `Barangay San Antonio Health Department - Statistics Report.xlsx` | Operation areas of the health center with who does them, whether the center controls them, and the known figures (patient load, charts, stock-outs) gathered from the interviews |

The formal requirements themselves are in **B2 Architecture Requirements Specification** (`Barangay San Antonio Health Department - Phase B (Business Architecture)`), including success measures SM-1 to SM-8. No patient names or records were collected.
