# Standards Library

Standards the architecture must comply with, identified from the interviews and verified with the health center.

| Standard | Type | Domain | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data Privacy Act (RA 10173) | Legal | Data, Application | Mandatory |
| DOH 7-year records retention | Legal | Data | Mandatory |
| PhilHealth YAKAP / GAMOT rules | Industry | Business, Application | Mandatory |
| DOH FHSIS reporting | Industry | Business, Data | Mandatory |
| NNC OPT Plus validity (≥80%) | Industry | Business | Mandatory |
| DOH sterilizer standard | Industry | Technology | Mandatory |
| City approval for system changes | Organizational | All | Mandatory |
| City software-installation rule | Organizational | Application, Technology | Mandatory |
| Equipment audit schedule | Organizational | Technology | Mandatory |

See also Section 4 of `Barangay San Antonio Health Department - Architecture Repository.pdf` in the parent folder.
